The Problem

A Data Desert Beneath Our Feet

Seventy percent of global agricultural land has no actionable soil monitoring. Farmers make critical decisions — planting, irrigation, fertilization — without the data that modern industry takes for granted. The result: wasted inputs, failed harvests, and a widening gap between what land could produce and what it does.

Climate volatility makes this worse every season. Traditional soil testing is slow, expensive, and local. Satellite data exists — but nobody has translated it into farm-level decisions. Until now.
