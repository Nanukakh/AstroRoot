The Challenge

The Global Freshwater Crisis

Agriculture consumes over 70% of the world's accessible freshwater. Accelerating climate change and systemic over-irrigation are depleting vital ecosystems and threatening global food security.

Traditional irrigation methods rely on outdated schedules or high-maintenance local sensors ,reactive systems in a world that demands foresight. The industry urgently needs predictive, data-driven, and scalable intelligence.
